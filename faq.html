CHEMICAL SAFETY, PETS & INDOOR PREPARATION

Yes. We use micro-encapsulated formulations and non-repellent transfer gels certified by the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) for domestic use. Once dried (typically within 90 minutes), the active molecules bind to surfaces and pose zero volatile threat to dogs, cats, or children.

No evacuation is necessary for cockroach micro-gel baiting or indoor barrier applications. You may cook, work, and sleep at home without interruption. For intensive whole-home thermal heat treatments for bed bugs or heavy sub-slab drilling, a brief 3 to 4 hour window is advised.

Zero staining and zero lingering chemical odor. We refuse to use cheap solvent-based organophosphates that leave an oily residue. Our aqueous suspensions dry invisible on marble, hardwood, tiles, and wallpaper.

TERMITES & STRUCTURAL BARRIER PROTOCOLS

Technicians use dustless 12mm masonry bits to drill holes at 300mm centers into foundation skirts or expansion joints. Termiticide is injected under pressure directly into the underlying soil to establish a continuous sub-slab curtain. Holes are then permanently sealed with color-matched polyurea compound flush with floor tiles.

The non-repellent active molecules bond to organic soil matter and have an empirical half-life exceeding 5 years. All full-perimeter Defendra treatments are covered by a written 5-Year Structural Retreatment Warranty.

Subterranean termites nest in ground soil and require soil moisture, accessing timber via mud tubes. Drywood termites live entirely inside dry timber (such as antique furniture or door leaves) and do not contact soil. Defendra provides sub-slab soil barriers for subterranean species and localized pressure injection or gas enclosure for drywood species.

COMMERCIAL WAREHOUSE & PORT FUMIGATION

Yes. Defendra holds active accreditation with the Indian Pest Control Association (IPCA Corporate Life Member #MH-4402). We provide statutory container sealing, post-clearance gas monitoring logs, and official Form 1-B certificates recognized by international customs and quarantine authorities.

Our commercial programs comply strictly with HACCP, BRCGS, and FSSAI audit standards. We deploy non-toxic mechanical exclusion, lockable digital pheromone monitoring stations, and nocturnal bio-enzymatic drain foaming, complete with full digital trend analysis logs for external health inspectors.
