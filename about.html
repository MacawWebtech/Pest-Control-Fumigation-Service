We don't mask pest activity. We remove the queen.

Ordinary pest control sprays repellent pyrethroids on visible walls. Foraging workers scatter, start satellite colonies inside the wall cavities, and the queen keeps laying hundreds of larvae a week.

We work the other way round. Acoustic radar finds the primary nest. Non-repellent transfer chemistry is carried to the queen by the workers themselves. Then structural barrier exclusion seals every entry point with marine-grade stainless mesh, so nothing comes back.

Fifteen years, four turning points

From a two-person entomology consultancy in Los Angeles to pest exclusion across six industrial corridors.

  1. 2011

    Founded in Los Angeles

    Two structural entomologists set up Defendra to bring odourless, micro-encapsulated termiticides to residential developments.

  2. 2015

    IPCA accreditation

    The Indian Pest Control Association granted Defendra life corporate membership for its port quarantine fumigation protocols.

  3. 2019

    Thermal heat pioneer

    First in the U.S. with 50°C convective heat remediation: a chemical-free, single-day bed bug treatment for luxury hotels.

  4. 2026

    Six-metro expansion

    71 GPS-tracked vans now cover Los Angeles, Chicago, Atlanta, Research Triangle, Miami and Phoenix, with a response time under 45 minutes.
