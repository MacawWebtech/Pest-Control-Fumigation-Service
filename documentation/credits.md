## 4. Design & Architectural Influences

The information architecture, visual hierarchy, and functional workflows of Defendra were inspired by industry-leading commercial pest control platforms:
- **Saela Pest Control (`saelapest.com`):** Modern industrial branding, compliance framing, and rigorous treatment timeline structure.
- **Orkin (`orkin.com`):** Information architecture, pest-specific taxonomy, guarantee structure, and multi-step conversion flow.
- **Hawx Pest Control (`hawxpestcontrol.com`):** Dynamic split hero banner composition for `index.html`.
- **Terminix (`terminix.com`):** Hero quick-quote estimate calculator composition for `index-2.html`.
