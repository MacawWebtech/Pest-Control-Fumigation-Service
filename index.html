SCIENTIFIC ERADICATION CYCLE

THE DEFENDRA 4-STAGE PRECISION PROTOCOL

Adapted from modern industrial entomology: we do not spray superficially. We locate the colony core, inject non-repellent transfer barriers, seal penetration pathways, and monitor via digital sensors.

STAGE 01

THERMAL & MOISTURE AUDIT

Calibrated acoustic probes and infrared moisture meters detect hidden subterranean nests within foundation slabs and wall voids without surface.

STAGE 02

TRANSFER CHEMISTRY

Micro-doses of non-repellent active agents are introduced. Worker insects unknowingly transfer active ingredients back to the breeding.

STAGE 03

STRUCTURAL EXCLUSION

Expansion joints, utility penetrations, and weep holes are hermetically sealed using heavy-gauge stainless mesh and elastomeric industrial sealants.

STAGE 04

365-DAY SURVEILLANCE

Quarterly sensor checkups and free retreatment calls ensure your facility or home remains completely sterile under our comprehensive warranty umbrella.
