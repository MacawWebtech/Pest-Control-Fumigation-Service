SUBTERRANEAN TERMITE DRILL-FILL-SEAL CHEMICAL BARRIER

A precision-engineered chemical envelope establishing an unbroken protective curtain along concrete foundations, perimeter footings, and structural masonry with a 5-year guarantee.

BIOLOGICAL MECHANISM

THE SCIENCE BEHIND NON-REPELLENT TRANSFER CHEMISTRY

Unlike outdated repellent organochlorine chemicals that merely divert foraging termites to unprotected gaps, Defendra employs advanced non-repellent fipronil and imidacloprid molecules. Subterranean worker termites cannot detect the active termiticide barrier.

As worker termites tunnel through the treated soil zone, micro-particles adhere to their exoskeletons. Through natural mutual grooming (trophallaxis) and food-sharing behaviors, the active agent is transferred back down to the reproductive king and queen, causing complete subterranean colony collapse within 21 to 28 days.

DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA

HOW TO IDENTIFY SUBTERRANEAN ACTIVITY

Termites consume timber from the inside out, often leaving behind only a paper-thin veneer of varnish or paint. Look for these four primary warning indicators:

Mud Shelter Tubes

Pencil-thick earthen tubes traveling along foundation walls, brick mortar lines, or sub-floor plumbing stacks used to maintain.

Hollow Wood Resonance

Baseboards, door frames, and architraves sounding noticeably hollow or papery when tapped with an inspection probe.

Discarded Alate Wings

Piles of translucent equal-length insect wings gathered on window sills or doorways following monsoon swarming flights.

Tight Door & Window Jams

Moisture introduced into timber framing by subterranean termites causes door jambs and wooden windows to swell and stick.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

THE 4-STAGE DRILL-FILL-SEAL TREATMENT EXECUTION

Before drilling a single hole, our senior entomologist maps sub-slab soil density, moisture pockets, and timber temperature variations using calibrated Termatrac radar and Fluke infrared imaging. This avoids hidden water lines and electrical conduits while identifying active foraging hubs.

Using heavy-duty dustless rotary hammer drills with HEPA vacuum collection, our technicians drill 12mm holes through the perimeter floor slab or masonry skirt at precise 300mm (12-inch) intervals, reaching down into the soil foundation beneath the building slab.

A calibrated sub-slab injection rod dispenses 5 to 7.5 liters of CIBRC-registered non-repellent termiticide emulsion per linear meter under 25 PSI pressure, creating a continuous, overlapping chemical envelope that seals every possible entry point.

Every drilled penetration is sealed flush with color-matched elastomeric polyurea masonry compound to match your existing floor tiles, marble, or exterior pavers. An official Defendra 5-Year Structural Retreatment Warranty Certificate is issued with GPS inspection coordinates.

PRE-TREATMENT CHECKLIST

WHAT TO PREPARE BEFORE TECHNICIAN ARRIVAL

  • Clear Perimeter Access: Move furniture and storage boxes at least 1.5 feet away from exterior-facing walls so technicians have unobstructed access to drill points.
  • Pet Relocation: Ensure indoor cats, dogs, and birds are secured in an untreated room or patio during active drilling and chemical injection.
  • Plumbing / Electrical Schematic: If available, share architectural layout drawings with the lead technician to verify sub-floor conduit alignments.
